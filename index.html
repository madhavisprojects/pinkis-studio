Agents & Models

AI agents we've built and run internally.

Automation agents used to power sales, support and operations across our own products.

AI Agent — Internal Tool

WhatsAppSalesAgent

Bulk outreach agent that sends personalized WhatsApp messages to a lead list via a connected WhatsApp account.

AI Agent — DevOps

DeploymentAgent

Handles Elastic Beanstalk deploys and account migrations across our production apps — verifies before it ships, never terminates a live environment without sign-off.

AI Agent — QA & Demos

DataSeedAgent

Loads realistic demo data — test profiles, shop categories, product catalogs with images — into our apps through their own APIs, for QA runs and client demos.

AI Model — Voice Companion

Aria

Hands-free cloned-voice AI companion inside ProTalk — replies in a member's own cloned voice when available, powered by real-time speech-to-text.

AI Agent — Internal Tool

LeadGenCRM

Finds and AI-scores local businesses from Google Places as onboarding leads for UrShop, with a CRM view to track outreach status.

AI Agent — Marketing

MarketingAgent

Scrapes a product's own website, then uses Gemini to generate on-brand social posts and promo videos — and publishes them straight to Instagram and YouTube on a schedule.
